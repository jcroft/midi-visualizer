River & Compass

Play along: A–K keys = C4–C5, Z/X octave, Space = sustain pedal

RiverNotes bloom at the light keyboard and flow left. Height is pitch, thickness is velocity, color is pitch class around the circle of fifths. Faint tails are notes the sustain pedal is still holding.

CompassTwelve pitch classes in circle-of-fifths order. Sounding notes light up and connect. A hollow ring marks a root you didn't play. The outer arc is the key; a dashed arc means a ii–V has implied a key that hasn't arrived yet.

Chord nameCalm on purpose: it changes only when a new reading holds for a moment. When a voicing is ambiguous, the runner-up shows faintly underneath.
